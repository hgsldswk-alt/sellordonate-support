Help

  1. Enter the expected sale price. Review the net amount after fees and shipping and compare the effective hourly rate with your own minimum.
  2. Add the item to your sell or donate list. Open List to change its destination, record actual proceeds, or mark it donated.
  3. Export CSV or open Share progress to prepare an image or share a settings code file. Use Import code to preview settings before applying them.
  4. In the paid edition, open Lifetime unlock to see the trial status, purchase price, and Restore purchases. The trial starts when you first open that edition. Basic calculations and your current list remain free, and past lists remain readable and exportable.

Privacy policy

The developer collects no data through the app. Calculations, lists, drafts and settings stay on your device. The app has no account, advertising or analytics. A settings code contains currency, an hourly minimum, selling time and an optional fee template. It contains no list or item details.

Apple handles purchases, restoration and rating requests through StoreKit. Files and images leave the app only when you explicitly share, export or copy them. The destination you choose handles the exported content.

To remove your on-device lists and settings, delete the app from your device. The local trial start may remain in the device Keychain for trial continuity after reinstallation.

卖还是捐 · 使用帮助

  1. 输入预计卖价，查看扣除手续费和运费后的净到手，并用自己的时薪门槛比较折算时薪。
  2. 加入待卖或待捐。打开清单可更改去向，记录实际净到账或标为已捐。
  3. 你可以导出 CSV，也可在「分享成果」生成图片或分享设置文件。从「导入口令」可先预览设置再应用。
  4. 收费版的「高级版买断」可查看试用状态、购买和恢复购买。首次打开收费版开始试用。基础计算和当前清单永久免费，历史清单始终可查看、导出。

隐私政策

开发者不通过本 App 收集数据。计算、清单、草稿和设置只存于本机。没有账号、广告或统计。设置口令只包含币种、时薪门槛、挂卖耗时和可选费率模板，不含清单或物品信息。

购买、恢复和评分由 Apple StoreKit 处理。你主动分享、导出或复制时，选择的接收方负责处理相应内容。

如需删除本机清单和设置，可以卸载 App。设备钥匙串可能保留试用起点，用于同机重装后延续试用。
